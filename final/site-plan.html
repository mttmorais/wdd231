South Florida Travel Guide

The name represents a information webpage focused in outdoor hints and destinations guide in the South Florida.

Purpose

The website promotes information about Florida's tourism. It gives travel tips, some information about destinations, outdoors activities and the current weather.

Scenario

➜ Where can I find information about natural springs, scenic destinations and outdoor activities?
